How this works

Win chances come straight from the Vegas spread. Tested on 2021-2025, a spread predicts winners exactly as well as the moneyline, and the numbers are honest: teams given 70-75% won 75% of the time. No model built from public stats reliably beats this, so we don't pretend to.

Most wins picks every favorite. That's the best play for season standings, especially in a small pool.

Win the week simulates your week thousands of times against the rest of your pool, assuming most people take favorites (and big favorites almost unanimously). If everyone has the same picks, nobody gains ground. Taking an underdog in a near coin-flip game costs you very little in expected wins but gives you a real chance to jump the field when it hits. The page only suggests flips that raise your odds of winning the week.

Balanced takes only the cheap flips: games where the underdog has at least a 40% chance, max two per week. A good default when there are both weekly and season prizes.

Tap any team to override a pick. Injury tags and wind are shown for context; Vegas lines already move for big news, so they aren't added on top.
